## Summary

The Component Library draws three things differently from the Components board. The templates
are full drawn cards and error callouts at the foot of a long page; the board shows them as a
strip of chips, one per file, each with its state. The Try it box says nothing about how it
works, and its error is a callout under the field; the board puts a short hint and the error on
the box's header line. A name clicked in the left list narrows the page but is not marked; the
board highlights the chosen name. This ticket brings the three in line with the board.

## Acceptance

The templates show as a chip strip under "Your templates", Try it's header carries its hint and
its error, and the chosen component is highlighted in the left list; shot in Marley Night and
Marley Day.
