cards or dashboard that allows you to view things based on rusty. do we remove the icons entirely on
the top left and force a home page or keep them?" Recommended one Rusty button and a home page; he
agreed ("a 1 time 2 click makes sense"), and on the cards: "we would want all the buttons up at the
top into the rusty home page card for now. we maybe have recents on the vault and then tasks table
etc just to have some visual." The rail's eight screen buttons become one Rusty button with a
placeholder icon, which opens a Rusty home tab, always first in the Rusty group: a card of every
screen's button, then the vault's recent pages, the open tasks as a table, and the decisions whose
follow-up is due.

pages, open tasks and follow-ups due, each row opening what it names.
